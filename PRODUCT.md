# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Content creators, editors, and site administrators. They need a high-fidelity, distraction-free environment to validate the visual impact and readability of long-form articles before publication.

## Product Purpose
The L.A.P. CMS serves as the backbone for a digital editorial platform. The preview page specifically exists to provide "live-view confidence," ensuring that typography, imagery, and markdown formatting are perfectly balanced for the end reader.

## Brand Personality
- **Sophisticated**: High-end editorial feel, utilizing premium typography and generous white space.
- **Intentional**: Every element has a place; no unnecessary clutter.
- **Cinematic**: Imagery is treated with respect, often serving as the emotional anchor of the content.

## Anti-references
- **Generic Admin Panels**: Avoid the "Bootstrap" or "standard SaaS" look (heavy borders, tight grids, gray-everything).
- **Distracting Toolbars**: The preview should focus on the content, not the CMS controls.
- **Cluttered Metadata**: Dates and authors shouldn't compete with the headline.

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Rhythm**: Use varying font weights and sizes to create a clear reading hierarchy.
- **Breathe**: Generous padding and margins to prevent cognitive load.
- **Material Fidelity**: The preview should be indistinguishable from the final live post, save for minimal administrative "status" markers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance for text contrast.
- Clear semantic heading structure for screen readers.
- Support for reduced motion in transitions.
